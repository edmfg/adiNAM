Strategic Takeaways for Adidas

  1. The Nike window is real but closing. Nike is in turnaround mode with a 15% marketing spend increase and renewed brand focus. Adidas should press the advantage now, especially in lifestyle/culture where Nike has lost ground.
  2. New Balance is the primary lifestyle/culture threat. Five consecutive years of 20%+ growth, private ownership patience, and strong collaborations make them the most dangerous competitor in the space Adidas Originals dominates.
  3. Performance running is crowded. On, Hoka, ASICS, and Brooks all have stronger running credibility. Competing here requires genuine product innovation, not just marketing.
  4. The retro silhouette boom benefits Adidas but carries risk. ASICS (Gel-NYC), New Balance (550/990), and Reebok are all playing the same heritage game. Samba/Gazelle fatigue is a real risk without lifecycle management.
  5. Puma's and UA's weakness = Adidas's opportunity. Both are in reset mode, creating shelf space and consumer attention gaps Adidas can fill — especially in wholesale.
  6. Apparel is underexploited. Lululemon is softening in NA while Vuori/Alo prove demand for premium athletic apparel. Adidas has the brand to compete but needs sharper product and positioning.

CULTURE

EASY TO MIND

Be unmissable across sports and the culture born from it. Deliver consistent icon strategy 2.0.

Challenge: 3x more brands activating in sports since 2023. Battle for visibility is tougher.
Signal: Super Bowl = 123.7M live views, but 13B addressable signals in communities around it
Lever: Earned and owned content propel the speed of culture. Even the Super Bowl had only a 3-day half-life.

COMMUNITY

IMPOSSIBLE TO MISS

Be impossible to miss at the moments our customers move into market. Turn consideration into purchase.

Goal: Close the conversion gap — adidas converts 33% of considerers vs 45% category average
Insight: Adidas buyers are 66% more likely to turn to social platforms for opinions vs category avg (55%)
Data: 7/10 streetwear choices directly influenced by creators. 76% trust UGC over traditional ads.
Signal: 84% of Billboard Global 200 songs in 2024 were TikTok famous first — communities drive discovery
Lever: Turn human communities into advocates → advocates lead to machine (LLM/AI) discovery

COMMERCE

EASY TO FIND

Be unmissable across the full commerce landscape. Move from performance to connected commerce.

The Strategic Thesis

Why this wins.

adidas is the only brand with the Relevance, the product breadth, and the community-first posture to credibly show up across all 7 growth spaces. The competitors can't replicate this because they don't have the breadth. And they won't invest in it because their models are built for mass, not for community.

⚡

Nike is losing Differentiation (96 to 76) and gaining Arrogance (85). Their model is top-down: signed athletes to mass campaigns. They don't have a community play.

⚡

New Balance wins on Authenticity (93) but plays it safe (Daring: 48). They're not going to show up at a Hyrox event or a slow-running collective. They don't take swings.

⚡

HOKA owns comfort-first running, but their cultural footprint is narrow. Not in pickleball, golf, gaming, or team sports. They can't go broad.

🏆

adidas has running + football + golf + tennis + Originals + training. 85M+ total addressable market. The 20% bet is a portfolio of culturally potent bets that collectively move Differentiation while growing the top line toward $10B.
