Strategic Priorities 5 priorities

▾
#What to DoWhy It Matters
1 Build a "youth + high school football" growth engine High school football is the largest boys' sport (~1.03M participants). High school sports at record ~8.27M. Massive addressable teamwear + training + spiritwear market with intense local identity.
2 Use MLS as a multicultural growth flywheel Hispanic fandom is younger, digital-first, more sponsor-responsive. MLS over-indexes in Hispanic avidity. Convert league partnership into creator-led, community-led drops.
3 Make NIL a scalable endorsement channel NIL structurally changes college athlete monetization and jersey commerce. Build a compliant NIL "roster + content + commerce" system. Exploit group licensing pathways.
4 Shift media/commerce to streaming-first Exclusive streaming games and league streaming packages are multiplying. RSNs are fragmenting. Buy/produce short-form + interactive content where rights are moving.
5 Canada: hockey + women's + bilingual Hockey is the national winter sport. 603K+ registered players. 15M Canadians reached for Stanley Cup G7. Women's hockey at record participation. Quebec-ready labeling mandatory.
