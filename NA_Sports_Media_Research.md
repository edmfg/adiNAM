# North America Sports & Media Landscape Research
## Adidas Media Strategy Pitch: Culture → Community → Commerce

# 1. SPORTS CULTURE UNIQUENESS IN NORTH AMERICA

## High School Sports as Community Identity

North America is unique globally in that high school sports serve as foundational community infrastructure. No other market has the equivalent of Friday Night Lights.

- **Record participation**: 2024-25 NFHS survey recorded an all-time high of **8,266,244 participants** — up 200,000+ from the prior year
- **Girls' sports driving growth**: Girls flag football surged **60% YoY** to 68,847 participants; girls wrestling topped **74,000 participants** for the first time (+15% YoY), adding ~1,000 new school programs
- **Community infrastructure role**: In rural states (Vermont 72%, South Dakota 69%, New Hampshire 68%), schools ARE the primary sports infrastructure, reducing cost barriers and making sport inseparable from local identity
- **Access gap reality**: Regular play disparity by income has widened to **20.2 percentage points** — a tension point for brands claiming community values

**Pitch implication**: High school sports is where brand identity forms. Adidas has an opportunity to be the brand of community sports infrastructure, not just elite performance.

---

## NCAA / NIL Ecosystem

The NIL (Name, Image, Likeness) revolution has created a marketing ecosystem that exists nowhere else in the world — 500,000+ college athletes who are simultaneously students, influencers, and brand ambassadors.

- **NIL is now a $1B+ endorsement economy** with the June 2025 House Settlement allowing universities to directly share revenue with athletes — **capped at $20.5M per program** in 2025-26, rising 4% annually
- **Deal distribution is heavily skewed**: 68% of NIL deals are worth **under $1,000**, but top earners command millions (AJ Dybantsa: $4.1-7.5M with Nike/Red Bull; Cooper Flagg: ~$6M in one season at Duke)
- **Over 60% of NCAA athletes** have received at least one NIL offer
- **Brand category growth**: Tech brands up **29% YoY** (EA Sports, Epic Games); non-alcoholic beverages up **19% YoY** — energy drinks and functional beverages align with Gen Z wellness positioning
- **Satisfaction gap = opportunity**: **58% of athletes** say at least one NIL deal "didn't deliver what was promised"; **59%** made less than expected — creating space for brands that build genuine, long-term partnerships
- **College athletes as influencers**: Some athletes are earning mid-six-figures in NIL and **delaying turning pro**, creating a longer window for brand-building relationships during their college careers

**Pitch implication**: The NIL space is flooded with transactional deals. Adidas can differentiate by building athlete development programs that create loyalty before these athletes go pro — the "Adidas family" approach.

---

## Women's Sports Explosion

This is not a trend — it is a structural market shift unprecedented in sports history, and it is uniquely accelerated in North America.

- **46 billion minutes** of women's sports consumed in the U.S. in 2025 (Nielsen)
- **WNBA**: Most-viewed full season ever on ESPN — averaged **1.2M viewers** (+5% YoY); regular season **1.3M** (+6% YoY); consumed for **220.12 million hours** (+16% YoY); Game 1 of Finals averaged **1.9M viewers** — most-watched Game 1 in 28 years
- **NWSL**: **61% increase** in average ESPN viewership; Championship averaged **1.18M viewers** (first NWSL final ever to surpass 1M), peaking at 1.55M; digital streams up **30% YoY**; record **2.62 billion minutes** watched across all platforms
- **WNBA media rights**: New 11-year deal with Disney/NBCU/Amazon worth **~$2.2 billion** (~$200M AAV) — nearly **4x increase** over prior deal; outpacing NBA's renewal rate by 2.6x
- **WNBA team valuations**: Average team now valued at **$269M**; Golden State Valkyries expansion team sold for **$500M**; NY Liberty valued at **$420M**
- **NWSL expansion**: Growing from 14 to 16 teams in 2026 (Boston, Denver); total team valuations predicted to increase from **$2.6B to $4.3B by 2027**
- **Viewer hours from women's pro leagues**: ~**370 million viewer hours** in 2024 — up **430%** from 2021

**Pitch implication**: Women's sports is the single biggest white-space opportunity for Adidas in NA. First-mover brand investment here builds permanent equity with the fastest-growing sports audience. The $2.5B monetization gap (McKinsey) represents underinvestment, not lack of demand.

## MLS Growth

MLS is the fastest-growing major sports league in North America and Adidas's most direct sports property connection.

- **Attendance**: 2025 regular season was second-highest in MLS history — **11.2M total fans**, average crowds of **21,988**; **19 clubs** averaged 20,000+ per match (more than any other soccer league globally)
- **Streaming viewership**: **3.7M gross live match viewers per week** across platforms (+29% YoY); Apple TV averaged **120,000 unique viewers per match** (+50% YoY)
- **MLS Cup**: Record **4.6M+ viewers** across all platforms; **70% of viewers under 45** — youngest-ever MLS Cup audience
- **Social/digital**: Fastest-growing major sports league across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and X
- **Star power multiplier**: Messi, Son Heung-Min driving casual viewership

**Pitch implication**: MLS is Adidas's home court. The young, digitally native, multicultural audience aligns perfectly with Adidas's brand DNA. The Apple TV partnership creates unique opportunities for integrated content-commerce experiences.

## Canadian Sports Culture

Canada represents a distinct market within NA with unique dynamics.

- **Hockey dominance**: **16.5M Canadian fans** — more than any other pro league in Canada; **66% identify as superfans/avid fans** (highest intensity of any league); average NHL fan in Canada is **49 years old** with **44% women** (unusually gender-balanced)
- **Hockey's linear TV stronghold**: NHL followers are far more likely to watch live, linear TV vs. other sports — keeping traditional broadcasters (Sportsnet, TSN) central. This creates a different media mix requirement than the U.S.
- **CFL momentum**: 2025 Grey Cup averaged **4.02M viewers** (+12% YoY); peak of **5.01M** (+11% YoY); strong growth in Vancouver, Toronto, Montreal (1.64M AMA, +10%, third consecutive year of growth)
- **Evolving landscape**: Basketball, soccer, baseball, and pickleball are rewriting Canadian participation trends, especially among younger demographics

**Pitch implication**: Canada requires a dual strategy — respect hockey's linear TV dominance while investing in the emerging digital-first sports (soccer, basketball) that index younger.

## Esports / Gaming Crossover

Gaming is not a separate silo — it is a sports consumption gateway for Gen Z.

- **Gen Z = 43% of global esports audience**; nearly **65% of viewers** aged 18-34
- **North America = 25.8%** of global esports market value at **$1.24B**; U.S. specifically generating **$338M in 2025**
- **42.8% of U.S. Gen Z** watch live streams regularly
- **70% of gamers play a sports game weekly** — massive crossover between gaming and sports audiences
- **Women = 28%** of NA esports viewing audience (growing)
- EA Sports and Epic Games are forming **joint sponsorships with NIL athletes** — blurring the line between real and virtual sports

**Pitch implication**: Every dollar spent on gaming-sports crossover reaches the exact demographic Adidas needs. The "athlete" in 2025 includes streamers, and the "stadium" includes Twitch and YouTube.

## Youth Sports Participation Trends

- Fastest-growing kids' sports for 2025-2026 include flag football (driven by girls' programs), pickleball, and running clubs
- **Widening income-based access gap** (20.2 points) creates both a challenge and a brand-purpose opportunity
- Rural communities rely more heavily on school-based sports, while urban youth face higher cost barriers through club/travel team models

# 2. SPORTS MEDIA LANDSCAPE SHIFTS (2024-2025)

## Creator-Led Sports Commentary Replacing Traditional Media

The intermediary between athlete and fan is disappearing. Athletes and creators ARE the media now.

- **USC Annenberg study**: **33 athlete-owned production companies** producing **370+ media properties** worldwide
- Athlete-owned podcasts: **7 billion YouTube views**, **725 million TikTok likes**, **37 million Instagram followers** combined
- **Mega-deals**: Kelce brothers' "New Heights" → **$100M deal with Amazon's Wondery**; Pat McAfee Show → **$85M ESPN license**
- **Global podcast industry**: Valued at **~$40 billion** by end of 2025
- **Video podcast explosion**: Spotify hosts **~500,000 video podcast shows**; **390 million users** have streamed a video podcast (+54% YoY)
- **Key shift**: When athletes speak directly into a mic for an hour, there's no editor, no filter, no network. The audience hears full context — this is why trust in creator media outpaces traditional outlets
- **Fan behavior evolution**: Individualization of fandom — fans follow athletes, not just teams — is accelerating

**Pitch implication**: Adidas's media spend should treat athlete-creators as primary channels, not secondary influencer buys. A $5M partnership with an athlete-media company (e.g., a podcast) may deliver more cultural impact than $20M in traditional sports broadcast ads.

---

## Streaming Wars for Sports Rights

Live sports is the last moat for linear TV — and it is falling.

- **2025 = the first year** where NFL and NBA fans can watch every single game via streaming — no pay-TV subscription required
- **Amazon Prime Video** becomes top global streaming sports rights spender in 2026, driven by its **$1.8B/year NBA deal** (11-year) + NFL Thursday Night Football + UEFA Champions League
- **Streamers will spend $14.2 billion on sports rights in 2026** (Ampere Analysis)
- Generalist streaming platforms (Amazon, Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV, Paramount+) will represent **44% of global streaming sports rights spending in 2026** (up from 31% in 2025)
- **Fan cost fragmentation**: NFL streaming costs reached nearly **$800 for 2025 season** (NFL Sunday Ticket + Amazon Prime + Netflix + Peacock)
- **Peacock** has essentially become a sports platform (NFL, NBA, MLB, college football, Olympics)

**Pitch implication**: The fragmentation of sports viewing creates a media planning nightmare but also an opportunity. Adidas can follow audiences across platforms rather than buying monolithic broadcast packages. Streaming also enables better attribution and interactive ad formats.

---

## Short-Form Sports Content & Highlight Culture

- Shorts, Reels, and TikToks approaching **2 billion users globally** each
- **60%+ of social media users** follow these platforms specifically for sports highlights, player updates, and match results
- **75% increase** in global short-form consumption
- Android users average **~35 hours/month** watching TikTok
- **Monetization gap**: Shorts creators earn **$0.01-$0.06 per 1,000 views** vs. **$1.25-$2.50** for long-form — engagement doesn't yet equal revenue
- Sports orgs have adopted more informal, authentic tone on TikTok — the "brand voice" of sport has shifted

**Pitch implication**: Short-form is where sports discovery happens. It's not where deep engagement or commerce happens (yet). Adidas should use short-form for top-of-funnel cultural relevance and funnel to longer-form content for conversion.

---

## Sports Betting Integration with Viewing

- **84 million adults** in U.S. and Canada participated in fantasy sports or sports betting in past 12 months (up from 81M in 2024)
- **77 million Americans**, **~9.3 million Canadians**
- **57 million** played fantasy sports; **66 million** bet on sports
- U.S. fantasy sports industry: **$10.4B revenue in 2025**, projected **$16B+ by 2030** (13.1% CAGR)
- Mobile applications = **76.7% of usage**, growing at 14.1% CAGR
- The 2018 Supreme Court ruling unleashed state-by-state legalization, blurring fantasy sports and traditional gambling lines

**Pitch implication**: Betting transforms passive viewers into hyper-engaged, data-obsessed participants. Every play matters to a bettor. This creates new attention windows for brand messaging — in-game, in-app, and in the group chat.

# 3. COMMUNITY DYNAMICS UNIQUE TO NA

## Team-Based Identity vs. Individual Sport Culture

North America is uniquely team-sport dominant (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, MLS, college) compared to Europe and Asia where individual sports (soccer club culture aside, tennis, F1, cricket) carry more weight. This creates:
- **Geographic tribal identity** (city/school-based)
- **Fantasy sports as community glue**: 57 million players creating micro-communities around draft picks, trades, and weekly matchups
- **Group chat culture**: Games are experienced through parallel text/social conversations. The second screen IS the primary social screen.

## NIL Collectives as New Community Structures

NIL collectives (fan-funded organizations pooling money for athletes) are creating new forms of sports community that bridge boosters, fans, and athletes in ways that didn't exist before 2021.

## Run Club Culture Boom

This is perhaps the most culturally significant community fitness trend in a decade.

- **59% increase** in running club participation in 2024 (Strava)
- **25% surge** in U.S. running clubs over the past five years (Running USA)
- **72% of Gen Z** join run clubs **to meet new people**
- **48% of people** say social connection is their top reason for joining a fitness group
- **Women joining running clubs on Strava rose 89% YoY**
- Club-affiliated runners log **78% more active time** than solo runners
- Group activities lead to **40% average increase** in activity length vs. solo
- Niche clubs emerging: trail runners, ultramarathoners, LGBTQ+ groups, Couch-to-5K beginners

**Pitch implication**: Run clubs are the new third place. They combine culture (identity, belonging), community (social connection), and commerce (shoes, apparel, accessories) in a single weekly touchpoint. This is Adidas's highest-potential owned community play.

---

## Women's Sports Fandom Communities

- Women's sports fandom is building its own distinct community ecosystem — not a subset of men's sports fandom
- WNBA, NWSL, NCAA women's basketball have distinct fan cultures with their own creators, podcasts, and social spaces
- Brands that treat women's sports as "also" rather than "first" will miss the authenticity required

## Sneaker Culture Communities

- **$6B U.S. sneaker resale market** in 2025; global sneaker market valued at **~$87B**
- StockX projected to lead with **$540.9M revenue** and **$3.8B valuation**
- Running silhouettes dominating: ASICS sales up **45% YoY** on StockX; Saucony +38%; Mizuno +148%; Brooks **+1,508%**
- Every major brand has embraced the slim sneaker wave (New Balance 204L now their #1 new model on StockX)
- **Only 47% of new releases** now trade above retail on secondary market (down from 58% in 2020) — the hype cycle is normalizing

**Pitch implication**: The sneaker resale market is maturing from hype to lifestyle. Running silhouettes are the new cultural currency. Adidas should lean into this shift (Samba success proves it) rather than chasing limited-edition hype drops.

# 4. COMMERCE & RETAIL TRENDS AFFECTING SPORTSWEAR (2024-2025)

## Social Commerce

- **TikTok Shop = ~20% of social commerce** in 2025; grew U.S. sales **108% YoY** to **$15.82B**; forecast to exceed **$20B in 2026** and **$30B by 2028**
- **53.2 million TikTok buyers** in 2025 (+13.6%)
- PUMA achieved a **737% revenue increase in ONE week** by combining LIVE shopping + creator affiliate programs on TikTok
- TikTok is the **#1 discovery engine** for Gen Z: used to create awareness and desire, while **physical stores remain preferred for purchase**

## DTC vs. Wholesale Strategy Rebalancing

- Both Nike and Adidas have **abandoned aggressive DTC-only targets** (Nike's 60%, Adidas's 50% goals by 2025 were never hit)
- Nike Direct reached ~**44% of Nike brand sales** in FY23; Adidas DTC at **39% in 2022**
- Industry consensus: brands are returning to a **balanced DTC + wholesale model** — recognizing that wholesale partnerships drive discovery and volume that DTC alone cannot replicate
- DTC operational costs (individual parcel shipping, returns handling, customer acquisition) make pure DTC unsustainable at scale

**Pitch implication**: The DTC vs. wholesale debate is over. The winner is **connected commerce** — seamless experience across channels with data flowing everywhere. Adidas's media strategy should drive to the most frictionless point of purchase, whether that's DTC, retail partner, or social commerce.

## Gen Z Shopping Behavior for Athletic Wear

- **90% of sportswear companies** predict steady or increased sales in 2025
- **2 in 3** Millennial and Gen Z customers wear athleisure **multiple times per week**
- Gen Z is **18% more likely** to buy products to "access the community built around them"
- TikTok + Instagram = discovery; **physical stores = purchase**
- Gen Z prioritizes: **comfort, sustainability, inclusivity** — in that order
- High-end athleisure brands (Alo Yoga) winning by combining **exclusivity + social media influence + status signaling**

## Sneaker Resale & Sustainability

- Global sneaker resale market projected to reach **$51.2B by 2032** (16.4% CAGR)
- **~30 million pairs** of sneakers thrown out annually in U.S.; **less than 5%** recycled
- Refurbished sneaker market growing as eco-conscious Gen Z and Millennials reframe secondhand as a fashion statement, not an economic compromise
- Nike Grind, Allbirds recycled materials, and similar programs gaining traction
- **1.2 billion pairs** of sneakers sold globally each year ($91.7B in sales)

## Influencer-Driven Commerce

- Athletes contributed **56% of total EMV** in sportswear industry
- **Adidas: $741M EMV** (vs. Nike $415M) — Adidas already leads here
- **81.5% of marketers** plan to increase or maintain influencer marketing budgets in 2025
- Live workout sessions on Instagram featuring product drops create FOMO-driven instant purchasing
- Ambassador programs (Gymshark model) build long-term community vs. one-off sponsored posts

# 5. EMERGING TRENDS THAT CREATE WHITE SPACE

## Pickleball & Emerging Sports

- **~22.7 million players** in U.S. by mid-2025 (+14.7% YoY); **36-38 million** played at least once during 2025
- **223.5% growth in three years**; **4th consecutive year** as America's fastest-growing sport
- **16,210+ places to play** (50%+ growth in facilities in 2024); **70,641 courts** in U.S.
- Every age group seeing increased participation — this is not just a boomer sport anymore
- Projected **15-20% annual growth** in 2025-2026

**Pitch implication**: Pickleball is an underserved category for performance sportswear. The community and social aspects (it's inherently a doubles/social sport) align perfectly with Adidas's culture-to-community thesis.

## Women's Sports Media Rights Explosion

(Detailed above in Section 1 — this is the single largest white-space opportunity)

- WNBA: **$2.2B media deal** (4x increase)
- NWSL: 61% viewership increase, expanding to 16 teams
- McKinsey: **$2.5B monetization gap** = the market is underfunding relative to audience demand

## College Athlete as Brand Ambassador

- Over **500,000 NCAA athletes** are now potential brand partners
- College athletes offer: authentic connection to fanbases, high engagement rates, content authenticity, large talent pool, lower cost than pro athletes
- Some athletes earning mid-six figures in NIL — **delaying going pro** to maximize college brand-building
- Creates a "draft pipeline" for brands: sign them in college, keep them through pro careers

## AI Personalization in Sports Retail

- Fitness app market projected to more than double from **$10.6B (2024) to $33.58B by 2033**
- **78% of AI features** in fitness apps are adaptive (learning user patterns)
- **51% of consumers** value personalized fitness experiences
- Connected fitness companies betting on hyper-personalization + community-driven engagement
- Gyms with strong community programs see retention rates **above 75%**

## Connected Fitness Communities

- Digital apps merging physical activity with real-life meetups
- Wellness tech shifting toward **supporting connection, not just performance**
- Echelon's AI-driven personalization drove **7% increase in retention**
- The opportunity is closing the loop: app → community → in-person meetup → product purchase

## Sports Tourism

- U.S. sports tourism market: **$65.4B in 2025**, projected **$227.6B by 2035** (13.3% CAGR)
- U.S. = **85.5%** of NA sports tourism market
- Marathon/destination race tourism growing — three-quarters of runners plan same or more races in 2025
- Adventure sports group tours gaining momentum among young travelers in U.S. and Canada

**Pitch implication**: Sports tourism creates high-intent, high-spend moments. Destination races, tournaments, and events are commerce moments where brand presence converts to purchase.

## Adaptive Sports Inclusion

- Global adaptive sports market: **$5.09B in 2024**, projected **$10.97B by 2032** (8.9% CAGR)
- Decathlon's adaptive equipment revenue doubling yearly; wheelchair sales surged **53% in H1 2024**
- Media coverage via Paralympics broadcasts improving visibility
- Brands using adaptive athletes as ambassadors of performance (Nike "You Can't Stop Us")
- **Key gap**: Adaptive equipment remains expensive; insurance often doesn't cover sports equipment — creating a brand-purpose opportunity for subsidized or sponsored gear programs

# KEY STRATEGIC THEMES FOR ADIDAS NA MEDIA PITCH

## Culture → Community → Commerce Framework

| Cultural Moment | Community It Creates | Commerce Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Women's sports explosion | Women's fandom communities (WNBA, NWSL) | Women's performance/lifestyle lines, media sponsorship |
| Run club boom | Local run communities (59% growth) | Running footwear, apparel, accessories, in-store events |
| NIL revolution | College athlete micro-communities | NIL ambassador programs, campus retail |
| MLS growth + World Cup 2026 | Soccer culture communities (multicultural, young) | Match-day apparel, streaming-integrated commerce |
| Pickleball emergence | Social sport communities | New category entry, court-side retail |
| Creator-led sports media | Podcast/content fandoms | Embedded commerce in creator content |
| High school sports | Small-town community identity | Team outfitting, grassroots brand building |
| Sneaker culture shift to lifestyle | Sneakerhead communities evolving toward running silhouettes | Resale ecosystem, lifestyle-performance crossover |

## What Makes NA Different (Executive Summary)

1. **No other market has high school + college sports** as a cultural institution — this creates a brand loyalty pipeline from age 14-22 that doesn't exist in Europe or Asia
2. **NIL is uniquely American** — 500,000+ college athlete-influencers are a media channel that only exists in the U.S.
3. **Sports media fragmentation is most advanced** in NA — streaming wars, creator economy, short-form highlights create the most complex (and opportunity-rich) media landscape globally
4. **Women's sports acceleration is fastest** in NA — viewership, investment, and cultural acceptance are 3-5 years ahead of any other market
5. **Community-as-identity** runs deeper in NA — from high school rivalries to fantasy leagues to run clubs, Americans build social identity around sports participation in ways that create natural commerce moments
6. **Social commerce maturity** — TikTok Shop, live shopping, creator affiliate programs are most developed in the NA market
7. **Sports betting integration** — 84M participants creates an entirely new engagement layer that doesn't exist at scale in most other markets
8. **Canada adds complexity** — hockey's linear TV dominance requires a distinct channel strategy; emerging sports (basketball, soccer) index younger and more digital
